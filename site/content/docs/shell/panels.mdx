Panels show what a view needs beside it, such as the selection's details, a terminal or logs.
The rail picks them.
A panel doesn't repeat the rail as tabs.

<Example id="panel/rail" />

## The rail

`PanelRail` is 44px wide at the window's right edge, on the canvas.
Its top group opens panels at the view's right, and its bottom group opens panels under the view.
One panel is open on each side at a time.
Each button has the panel's name and shortcut in its tooltip.

The panels on offer come from the lens, then the scope, then every registered panel.
A lens can open panels the first time it is put on, with `open`.

## The docked panel

`ShellPanel` is docked inside the view's raised surface, square like it.
Its 44px header holds, from left to right:

- the panel's name, or its trail once it drills
- its real content tabs, when it has them
- its actions
- close, also Escape inside the panel, and ⌘J for the bottom panel

There is no split or tiling button.

| Panel | Size | Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Right | 340px wide | 280px to 520px |
| Bottom | 280px tall | 140px to 600px |

Both sizes are kept in the shell's storage.
The right panel floats over the view when docking it would leave the view narrower than 480px.

## Real tabs

A panel's tabs are its own content, such as a terminal's sessions.
They come from the panel's `tabs`, which is called with `{ shell }` like `render`, and use `TabStrip` with full tab management:

- drag to reorder, double-click or Rename to rename
- × or a middle click to close, and Close others and Close to the right in a tab's menu
- ⌃Tab and ⌃⇧Tab to cycle, ⌘T for a new tab
- an overflow menu listing every tab when they don't fit
- a status dot per tab: running, waiting for input, exited, or failed with its code

## Covers

A place's `covers` names the panels whose content its view already is.
On a host's Sessions view, which is the terminal, the rail hides the Terminal panel.
A panel open at the time is hidden while the view shows, and comes back when the person leaves it.
No surface shows what another already shows.
A function in place of the list gets the record shown, for a place whose record page shows what its list needs a panel for.
A <DemoLink to="agent/chats/c-arm64">conversation</DemoLink> shows its context and output rate beside the transcript, so its Details panel steps aside there and comes back on the list.
Open the Terminal panel on <DemoLink to="hosts/build-01/operate/logs">a host's logs</DemoLink>, then go to <DemoLink to="hosts/build-01/operate/sessions">its sessions</DemoLink>.

## A panel's drill

A panel can drill from its subject to a linked record with `shell.drill("details", "WEB-127")`.
Its header then shows its trail, `WEB-139 / WEB-127`, and the view doesn't move.
The drill is part of the address.
It is a stop in the history, it is kept on reload, and it can be linked.
Selecting another row starts the panel over from that row.
Escape goes up the drill one level.

## Resizable borders

Every border between columns resizes with `ResizeHandle`, including the sidebar's edge, the panels' edges and the borders inside a view.

- It is invisible until hovered for 120ms, with a 4px hit area over the hairline.
- Only the handle being dragged lights up.
- The arrow keys move it when it has focus, with Shift for larger steps, and Home and End go to the limits.
- A double click resets it.
- `createStoredWidth(id, { initial, min, max, storage })` keeps each width by id.
