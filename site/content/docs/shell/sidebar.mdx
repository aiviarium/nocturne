The sidebar shows the places of the scope the window is in.
Going into a scope, such as a project or a host, replaces the tree with that scope's places.
The title bar's path names the scope, so the sidebar doesn't.

<Example id="navigation/fold-rail" />

In the demo, <DemoLink to="hosts/build-01/operate/overview">a host</DemoLink> drills the sidebar into its places.

## The header

`SidebarHeader` is a 44px row, from left to right:

1. ↑, which goes up one level. At a record it goes to the record's list, "Up to Issues". Elsewhere it goes to the scope above, "Up to Projects". With nowhere up it is disabled, and the filter field beside it keeps its position.
2. The filter field, which fills the free width.
3. Collapse all, when the tree has parents.
4. Minimise, which folds the sidebar to its rail.

The title bar's path names the scope, and the header leaves it out.
The field is quiet at rest and 28px tall, with 12px text.
⌘F or / focuses it.
Typing filters the tree in place.
Matches and their parents stay, with the match underlined in `text-3`.
Escape clears it.
Type in the filter on <DemoLink to="projects/atlas/build/issues">Issues</DemoLink> to try it.

## The tree

`Nav` draws the tree at the drill size.

| What | Size |
| --- | --- |
| Row | 30px |
| Label | 12px/500 |
| Icon | 15px, `text-3` at rest |
| Gap between icon and label | 8px |
| Count | 11px, plain `text-3` figures |

A child view starts at its parent's label, 8 + 15 + 8px from the row's edge.
The current item takes the `active` fill and `text-1`, at the same size and weight.
Hover only lightens the label and the icon.
A count shows above zero.
On hover, a chevron at the row's end takes the count's place and folds the parent's children.
The parent's icon stays.

A place with `enters` lists scopes: opening one of its rows nests that scope in the path, and the tree drills into it.
Entering a scope opens it as the person left it, with the lens worn last and the place that lens remembers.

## Entries

A place with few entries lists them under its row with `entries`. The person opens one without going through the list.
The place's row still opens its list.
Its records open in place, and the scopes it `enters` drill in, with a chevron.
The open entry is marked in place of its place.
Past the `limit`, 8 by default, the rest fold into one row, "Show 3 more", which lists them in place.
That row is centred, in 11px `text-4`, and 24px tall.
The list then ends in "Show fewer".
The shell keeps which lists are open in its storage, and the open entry shows when its list is folded.
A long or filtered list, such as issues, is left to its view.
A right click, the Menu key or Shift and F10 on an entry opens the same menu as its row in the list: the place's `menu` for a record, the scope's `menu` for a scope.
The root's groups of projects and hosts open their scope's menu too.
`NavItem` takes the menu as `menu`.

On <DemoLink to="projects/atlas/build/pulls">a project</DemoLink> the open pull requests are listed under Pull requests by title, and on <DemoLink to="hosts/build-01/operate/overview">a host</DemoLink> the sessions, three before "Show 2 more", and the latest deploys.

## The icon rail

Minimise, ⌘B or ⌘\\, or a drag of the edge below 140px folds the sidebar to a 48px rail.

- The tree's top-level places show as 32px icon buttons with their names in tooltips.
- A place whose children count shows a neutral dot.
- A section shows as its place's icon alone. Pressing it runs the group's `open`, or its `onClick` without one, and the icon is marked while the place is open.
  Hovering or focusing the icon shows the section's entries in a flyout to its right.
  The small chevron under the icon lists them on the rail.
  The flyout's heading has the section's `actions` too.
- The account shows as its avatar at the foot.
- The header is one button where ↑ was, "Show sidebar" (⌘B), which restores the last full width. A drag of the rail's edge past 140px does the same.

Below 900px window width the sidebar starts folded, and unfolding opens it until the window widens.
`foldBelow` changes the width, and 0 turns it off.
The fold and the width are kept in the shell's storage.

## The account

The sidebar's foot is an `AccountMenu`.
It says who is signed in, holds the app's items, "Appearance…" with `onAppearance`, the theme as one row of three icons, and sign out last.
Folded, it is the avatar alone.

## Group headings

A `NavGroup` heading, such as Projects in the root, is 11px `text-3`.
It may start with a 13px `icon` and end with icon `actions`, such as making a new item and opening the group's list.
An action with `current` is marked like a current row, such as a section's open button while its list is in the view.
With `collapsible` it ends in a chevron that folds the group, kept in the same `collapsed` list as folded parents under the group's `id`.
A `footer` is a row under the group's items for its own controls, such as making a new item, and it shows while the group is folded.
A group with the open item in it can't fold.
A heading with `onClick` stands for its place, such as Sessions over a host's sessions, and the place has no other row.
Clicking it opens the place, `count` follows the label, and `current` gives it the `active` fill.

## Drill or nest

Drill into a scope when the target is a domain of its own, with its own lists, panels and actions.
Projects drill to a project, and the fleet to a host.

Nest in place when the data is a part of one view.
Saved views are child rows under their place, and a page tree stays a tree.
Choosing an item in place changes the view and keeps the tree.
