The shell is the window around every view.
An app registers its scopes, lenses, places, records and panels with `createShell`, and `<Shell>` draws the window from them.
The app writes its views and leaves the frame to the shell.

<Example id="app-shell/workbench" />

The <DemoLink to="projects/atlas/build/issues">demo</DemoLink> is a whole app on the shell, at full size.

## The layout

| Region | What it holds | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title bar | The app icon, the history arrows, the scope path, the lens box on the window's centre, status items and the window controls | 44px across the window |
| Sidebar | Its header, the current scope's places, the account at its foot | 240px, 200px to 360px, folds to a 48px rail |
| View surface | The view, with its own 44px header | Fills the rest, down to the window's bottom edge |
| Docked panels | One panel at the view's right and one under it | Resized by their edge |
| Panel rail | The buttons that open the panels | 44px at the window's right edge |

The view area is raised on the canvas.
It and its docked panels are square, because viewport containers have no radius.
A rounded corner would clash with the title bar's line.
Buttons, fields, chips, cards, selection fills and everything that floats keep their radius.

The title bar, the sidebar header, the view header and the panel headers are all 44px.

## Structure and time

The view and each panel show where they are, as a trail in their own header, and every crumb goes up.
Where the person was is kept once, for the whole window, in the [history](./history.mdx).
No view or panel has a back button of its own.

## No flicker

- Switching a place, a lens, a record, an inbox item, a tab or a panel's drill replaces the content in the same frame. Content swaps never fade.
- The frame, the sidebar and the panels are never remounted. They take new data. A view mounts once per scope, lens and place, and a record or a drill swaps its data only.
- No transient fallback shows between two states, and the layout settles in the frame the address changes.
- Only things that appear fade in, such as menus, popovers, the palette, dialogs and toasts. See [Overlays](../overlays.mdx).

## No network code

The shell doesn't touch the location bar, the browser's storage or the native window.
They come in through adapters the app passes.

| Adapter | Built in | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Router | `memoryRouter()`, `hashRouter()`, or the app's own `HistoryRouter` | [History](./history.mdx#routers) |
| Storage | `webStorage(localStorage)`, `memoryStorage()`, or the app's own `ShellStorage` | [Registering](./create-shell.mdx#storage) |
| Window | The handlers of `WindowControls`, passed as `window` | [Title bar](./title-bar.mdx#window-controls) |
