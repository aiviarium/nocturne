title: Appearance
description: The theme, the accent that follows lenses, and the tint line, applied on the root element.
---

`createAppearance` keeps three choices and applies them at once.
`AppearanceSettings` draws them as Settings › Appearance.

| Setting | Values | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theme | System, Light, Dark | The theme the root has until a choice is made, or `defaultTheme` |
| Accent follows lens | On or off. Off, the base accent everywhere | On |
| Lens tint line | On or off. Off, the plain hairline under the title bar | On |

## Where it applies

The appearance writes `data-theme` and the lens's accent on `<html>` by default, or on the `root` it is given.
Overlays render in the top layer and inherit both.
A menu takes the accent of the window.

`--accent` is a registered property, so a lens switch cross-fades the accent in 100ms.
Under reduced motion it changes at once.
A scope without lenses uses the base accent.
See [Theming](../theming.mdx#the-accent).

## Where it is opened

- Settings › Appearance in the app.
- "Appearance…" in the account menu, with `onAppearance`.
- The account menu's theme row sets the same theme choice.

## Wiring

```tsx
const storage = webStorage(localStorage)
const appearance = createAppearance({ storage })

<Shell shell={shell} appearance={appearance} />
```

The choices are kept in the `storage` passed, under `appearance`.
Without one they last as long as the page.
`Shell` passes the appearance to the title bar, whose lens box makes the accent follow the lens that is on.
