## The focus ring

Every interactive element shows the focus ring, `--focus-ring`, on keyboard focus.
The ring is a 1px line in `--accent-strong` with a 3px spread of the accent around it.
It follows the accent, so it takes the hue of the lens that is on.
`bundle.css` derives `--focus-ring-inset` from the same values.
The parts draw it themselves.
A global rule in `src/styles/parts.css` gives it to any other button, link, tab or field inside `.nc`.

Do not set `outline: none` without a replacement.

- The global rule leaves out parts that draw their own focus. Fields show it on their edge and menus with their highlight. A list row draws the ring around the full row.
- Inside a list, a grid, a tree, a tab list or breadcrumbs, the ring is drawn inside the element with `--focus-ring-inset`. Drawn outside, the edge of the list would cut it off.
- Focus scrolls a little past an element, so the ring is not cut at the edge of a scroll area.
- A separator, such as the divider of a `Split`, shows focus by its colour.

The ring values are on [Shadows](./tokens/shadows.mdx).

## Keyboard

Tab through the <DemoLink to="settings/general">General settings</DemoLink> of the demo to see the ring on each control.

- Lists and tables are one tab stop. The arrow keys, Home and End move between rows.
- Menus move with Arrow Up and Down, which wrap, and with Home, End and typing a letter.
- A `Dialog` moves focus in when it opens. Tab cycles inside, Escape closes it, and focus returns to the button that opened it.
- Escape closes only the topmost open layer. Inside the app shell it then walks the shell's own stack, see [Keyboard](./shell/keyboard.mdx#escape).

## Contrast

`text-3` is the faintest text colour for anything a person reads.
`text-4` is for disabled and decorative marks only, such as separators and the dash of an empty value.
Status text uses the `-ink` shades, see [Colour](./tokens/colour.mdx#text).

## Hit areas on touch

On a phone the smallest hit area is 44px.
The visible control may be smaller.
