Each swatch is drawn over `bg-1` of its theme.
Tints show as they look on a card.

## Surfaces

Neutral greys with no tint.
`bg-0` is the canvas, `bg-1` the frames and cards, and `bg-2` fields and nested cards.
`bg-3` is hover and the button fill, and `bg-4` is selection.

## Hairlines

`line-subtle` divides table rows, `line-soft` edges cards and code, `line` edges controls, and `line-strong` is the strongest edge.

## Text

<ColourTable prefix={['text']} />

`text-3` is the faintest colour for anything a person reads.
`text-4` is for disabled and decorative marks only, such as separators and the dash of an empty value.
Status text uses the `-ink` shades of `extra.css`: `--success-ink`, `--warning-ink` and `--danger-ink` mix the status colour with `text-1`.

## Neutral fills

Cards, panels, rows, strips, banners and callouts are the neutral `bg-*` steps with hairlines.
Hue goes in the foreground, such as an icon, the dot and word of a `State`, or the ink of text.

- A small inline chip or badge may keep a slight tint with a stronger ink of the same hue.
  `Badge`, `LabelChip`, the composer's tokens and the "Modified" chip do this, as the mention chip does.
- Coloured fills are for icon tiles and buttons: an accent or danger button, the tile behind an icon.
- No hue fill has a hue edge, and a danger button has no red edge.
- A hue edge is never the only mark of a state on a card. Approval cards have a hairline edge, and show their state with a dot and a word.
- Selection and hover are neutral fills, `bg-4` and `bg-3`.
  So are the selected rows of a table and its bulk bar, a running tool step and the composer while the assistant works.
  A running step has the hue in its text.

`bundle.css` still has status tints behind callouts, approval cards, the bulk bar, selected table rows and running steps.
It also has hue edges around danger buttons and stream pills.
`src/styles/parts.css` overrides those rules in its colour section.
