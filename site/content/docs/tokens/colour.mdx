Each swatch is drawn over `bg-1` of its theme.
Tints show as they look on a card.

## Surfaces

Neutral greys with no tint.
`bg-0` is the canvas, `bg-1` the frames and cards, and `bg-2` fields and nested cards.
`bg-3` is the button fill, and `bg-4` the fill of key caps, counts, avatars and meter tracks.
The view area is raised on the canvas, and headers and list group heads have no fill.

<ColourTable prefix={['bg']} />

The state fills come from `text-1`.

<ColourTable names={['hover', 'active', 'press']} />

- `hover` is behind an icon-only button on hover, to show its hit area.
- `active` is selection and the current item.
- `press` is the fill while pressed.

Rows, sidebar items, chips and text buttons take no fill on hover: only their text and icons lighten.

## Hairlines

Lines are translucent white in dark and a translucent near-black in light, and look the same on every background.
`line-subtle` divides table rows, `line-soft` edges cards and code, `line` edges controls, and `line-strong` is the strongest edge, such as the ring of a selected card.

## Text

<ColourTable prefix={['text']} />

Text selection is neutral: `--selection` is `text-1` at 22% in both themes, and the caret is `text-1`.
Neither takes the accent or a lens's hue, in fields, the palette, the composer, the terminal or prose.

<ColourTable names={['selection']} />

`text-3` is the faintest colour for anything a person reads.
`text-4` is for disabled and decorative marks only, such as separators and the dash of an empty value.
Status text uses the `-ink` shades of `extra.css`: `--success-ink`, `--warning-ink` and `--danger-ink` mix the status colour with `text-1`.

## Primary

The primary button is ink, filled with the text colour in both themes and whatever the lens.
The composer's send button is drawn the same way.

## Accent

`--accent` is the one colour an app sets, see [Theming](../theming.mdx#the-accent).
The values here are Iris, the default.
The accent follows the hue of the lens that is on.

The accent is used for these only:

- focus rings, including the palette's 3px glow around its field
- the icon of the lens that is on
- the tint line under the title bar
- checked switches and checkboxes
- text links

It never marks selected rows, active tabs or nav items, counts and badges, unread dots, tags, progress, toggled buttons, menu checks, filter matches, drop lines or hover.
Those are neutral, or take the status colours the data needs.

## Neutral fills

Cards, panels, rows, strips, banners and callouts are the neutral `bg-*` steps with hairlines.
Hue goes in the foreground, such as an icon, the dot and word of a `State`, or the ink of text.
The greys have no tint, with equal red, green and blue, and no background is filled with the accent.
`bun run lint:tokens` fails on a tinted grey token and on a background filled with `--accent-tint`.

- A small status chip may keep a slight tint of its status hue with a stronger ink of the same hue.
  An accent badge, the composer's command token and a running step are neutral, with the accent in their text.
- Coloured fills are for icon tiles and buttons: an accent or danger button, the tile behind an icon.
- No hue fill has a hue edge, and a danger button has no red edge.
- A hue edge is never the only mark of a state on a card. Approval cards have a hairline edge, and show their state with a dot and a word.
- Selection is the neutral `active` fill with a step up in text colour, from `text-2` to `text-1`. It never adds a bar or an underline, and never changes the size or the weight. Hover on rows, sidebar items and text buttons only lightens the text.
- A selected card takes a `line-strong` ring.
- The selected rows of a table, its bulk bar, a running tool step and the composer while the assistant works are neutral too. A running step has the hue in its text.
