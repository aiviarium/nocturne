---
title: Shadows
description: The overlay shadow, the focus rings and the glows.
---

Nocturne has few shadows.
Surfaces are separated by their grey steps and hairlines.
A shadow marks what floats, the ring marks keyboard focus, and the glows mark what moves.

<ShadowTable />

| Token | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `--shadow-overlay` | Menus, tooltips and the card being dragged on a board |
| `--focus-ring` | Keyboard focus on any interactive element, see [Focus](../focus.mdx) |
| `--focus-ring-inset` | Focus on tabs, sidebar items and the tab strip, drawn inside the edge |
| `--glow-live` | The dot of a live state |
| `--glow-ai` | The dot of the assistant's state |

Only `live` and `ai` states glow.
The moving part of a `Widget` has the hue and its glow.
