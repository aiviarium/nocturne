## Board

Use `Board`, `BoardLane` and `BoardCard` for records in lanes.

- Lanes are 272px wide and never squeezed, and the board scrolls sideways when they do not fit.
- The head of a lane is 30px high. It has a dot or icon, the name, a count and an add control, and it highlights when a card is dragged over it.
- A card is a fixed 112px slot. It has a top row with a key and a person, a title cut to two lines, and the meta row on its bottom edge. `BoardCardTop`, `BoardCardTitle` and `BoardCardMeta` build the card of an entity.
- In a board view, `fill` makes lanes scroll inside, and `placed` cards let a lane render only what is visible.
- In a document, lanes are as tall as their cards.
- Below 560px of the board's own width the board is grouped lists with 44px rows. This is a container query, and `useBoard().stacked()` reports it.
- A stacked card with a top row keeps the desktop order. The key is at the start of the first row and the person at its end. The title is under them, and the labels are at the bottom.
- Selection is a neutral fill, and the cursor is the focus ring.

## DataTable

Use `DataTable` for a table of records.
Rows are 30px, and columns can sort.

- `selectable` works as in a list. A click opens a row, and a click with Cmd, Ctrl or Shift selects. Checkboxes show on hover, on focus, and while something is selected. There is a select-all box. Selection is a neutral fill. The toolbar becomes the bulk bar only while something is selected.
- `onReorder` moves rows. Drag the row itself, or use Alt and an arrow key. A line shows where it drops, and the table scrolls at its edges. It works only while the table is not sorted by a column.
- `onRemove` removes rows, with Undo.
- `rowMenu` is the row menu, as a `RowList` has.

The table is one tab stop, and the arrow keys move between rows.
Use a `RowList` for a list of things with one title each.

## DataList

Use `DataList` for records edited in place.

- Tags and URLs, with one field.
- Environment variables, with `fields` for a name and a value.
- Any record of a few short fields.

The compact form goes in a form (`boxed`, a box like a field) or in an inspector (`inline`, rows on the edge).
`variant="table"` adds a header row, for many records.

It selects, reorders, removes with Undo, adds, and moves by keyboard as a `DataTable` does, with the same row menu.
In a field, Enter, Backspace and the arrow keys work on the caret.
Escape goes to the row.

On a phone it is an iOS form group.
`SchemaForm` uses it for arrays of strings and of small records.
Records that open are a `RowList` with `onAdd`, not a `DataList`.
