Use a widget for one live value in a small space, such as a dashboard cell or the Details panel beside an agent's chat.
A `Stat` reports a number once, and a widget follows a value as it changes.
Most of it is the live part. The words are a title, a value and a unit.

In the shell a page of widgets is a view of its own, on a `WidgetGrid`.
One widget on its own goes in a panel or a card of a view, at the size that fits there.

## Widget

- Radius 20, padding 14px by 16px.
- One hue, the accent or a status colour. Only the moving part has the hue and its glow.
- Parts of one whole and side-by-side readings take a colour each, from the label palette, so neighbours differ.
  This holds for `WidgetSegments` and `WidgetMeters`. A part can set its own `hue`.
- A chart of recent values draws every value the same way. The newest value is not marked apart.
- The head is one row. The title, or a `figure` with its `unit`, sits at the left and the value at the right.
  Use a `figure` when the unit says what the reading is, as in 82 mmHg or 48 tok/s. Give the widget a `label` then.
- Put labels under a chart in `axis`, not in a sentence. A `caption` is one line and is optional.
- With `fill` in a grid cell, the head is at the top and a sparkline or list grows to fill the cell. A part that
  cannot grow goes at the bottom with its axis.

## Live parts

| The live part | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `WidgetProgress` | Progress. `start` and `end` name its ends. |
| `WidgetSteps` | A run of stops, such as a plan or a pipeline. |
| `WidgetSegments` | What fills a context window or a disk. `labels` sets where the names go. |
| `WidgetSparkline` | Recent values. `variant="wave"` for a rate or a level. |
| `WidgetGauge` | A share of a bound, as a shallow arc. |
| `WidgetMeters` | A few readings of one machine: CPU, memory, disk. |
| `WidgetRing` | A share in a `row` widget: context used, time left. |
| `WidgetUptime` | A strip of days. |
| `WidgetTimer` | Time left or elapsed. |
| `WidgetControls` | The icon buttons of a timer or a run, at the widget's foot. |
| `WidgetStats` | A few readings under the chart of a large widget: p50, peak. |

## Sizes

A widget can show the same thing at more than one size, as on a phone's home screen.
It offers the sizes that suit it and does not have to offer them all.

| Size | Shows | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `row` | The title and the value on one line, after a ring, icon or small wave | A status column or strip |
| `sm` | The head and a slim live part, without names | A side panel short on height |
| `md` | The head and the live part. The default. | A side panel, a dashboard cell |
| `lg` | The title above a larger figure, a taller chart, and the detail | A dashboard |

At `sm` a part leaves out its names. They stay in its accessible name.

## ContextWidget and RateWidget

`ContextWidget` and `RateWidget` are ready-made, at the sizes that suit them.

- `ContextWidget` shows an agent's context window. At `row` it is a ring and the share. At `sm` it is the
  share over one bar. At `md`, the default, it is used of total over the bar, with a legend of the parts. At `lg` it is the share, used of total, the bar, and a row per part with its
  size and share.
- `RateWidget` shows a rate, such as tokens per second. At `row` it is a small wave, the title and the rate. At
  `md`, the default, it is the rate and one reading over the wave. At `lg` it adds a taller wave, its axis and
  the readings.

## WidgetGrid

`WidgetGrid` is a page of widgets: 12 columns of 80px rows.
While `editing`, items drag and resize.

Outside editing it draws the stored layout compacted.

- Items in `empty`, with nothing to show, take no space.
- Each item is as tall as its content, up to its size. This is `fit`, on by default.
- Items of one height on one row end on one line.
- Everything moves up.

Cards on a `WidgetGrid` take the widget frame, with 18 or 20px padding and radius 20.
