A conversation with the assistant is a view of its own, such as a chat at a record of the Chats place, or the Assistant panel beside any view.
The messages fill the column and the `Composer` sits at its foot.
Code, diffs and logs go in a view, a panel or a message, at the full width of their column.

## ChatMessage

Use `ChatMessage` for each message of a conversation.
The person writes in a bubble, and the assistant writes on the page.
Messages aren't indented.
The avatar and the thought head the reply on a line of their own, and the reply under them takes the full width of the column.
- `ToolSteps` holds the assistant's tool steps, and each `ToolStep` is a row that expands into its output.
- The running step glows, with its hue in its text, and finished steps don't.
- A decision is an `ApprovalCard`. It has a warning dot and a one-line summary, then the affected items, with the choices at the bottom right. Its edge is a hairline, and its state shows in its dot and word.
- A `Token` is an inline chip for a picked command, reference, skill or person, in a message and in the composer.

## Media

Put images, audio, video and files in a `ChatMedia` in the message's children.
It takes a list of items, each with a `kind` and a `src`.
The source is a URL or an object URL, and the part doesn't fetch anything.

Media fill the width of the column, as the reply does.

- One image takes the full width at its own aspect ratio, up to 480px high.
  Pass `width` and `height` so the space is kept before it loads.
- A pair of images sit side by side.
  Three or four form one row, and more show four with the count of the rest on the last.
- Video takes the full width too.
- Audio and file cards are smaller, with the same box and height. They share rows that wrap, and grow to fill each row.
- An image shows a shimmer while it loads, and a message with its alt text when it fails.
- An item with `pending` is still arriving.
  It shows a shimmer at its final size, so the message doesn't jump when it arrives.
- Audio is a card with a play button, a static wave and the time.
- Video uses the browser's controls, with a `poster`.
- A file is a card with its name, type and size.
  Its action downloads the `src`, or calls `onOpen` when you pass it.

A click on an image opens `MediaViewer`, a dialog with the image large.
The arrow keys step between images, Escape closes it and focus returns to the image.
On a phone a sideways swipe steps between images, and pinch zoom works as in the browser.
`MediaViewer` is exported for images outside a message.

## Composer

Use `Composer` for the input to the assistant, at the foot of a chat or the Assistant panel.
It is a `contenteditable` field, and its send button is ink like a primary button.

- `/` at the start of a line picks a command.
- `@` adds context.
- `$` adds a skill.

Picked commands, mentions and skills are atomic chips.
The value is a list of text and chip segments, `ComposerValue`.
The component only exposes callbacks.
It doesn't talk to the network or the microphone.
Dictation comes in through `TranscriberProvider`.
A transcriber whose microphone is blocked rejects with `DictationBlockedError`, and the composer shows the blocked notice.
`DictationBar` draws the same recording state for an input of your own.
While recording, the bottom row shows the red dot, the time and the wave, up to Cancel and Finish.
The wave fills that space, with the newest level at the end.
The text heard so far sits where the typed text goes.
A notice about the input, such as a blocked microphone or a rate limit, sits in a strip on the top edge of the composer, outside its box.
Pass your own as `notice`.
An attachment with `kind: "image"` and a `preview` URL shows as a thumbnail with a remove button.
Other attachments are chips.
A file pasted or dropped into the composer, such as a screenshot, goes to `onAttachFiles`, and the app adds it to `attachments`.

## StreamState

`StreamState` is the connection state of a stream, such as a log tail or a running reply.
Its states run from connecting and live to paused, dropped, ended and error.
Put it in the header of the panel or frame, next to the title.

## CodeBlock

`CodeBlock` shows code with line numbers and the colours of the `syn` tokens.
The bar shows the file name, with Copy and an optional Apply.
Use it for a file or a snippet, in a view or a message.

## LogViewer

`LogViewer` is a scrolling mono log for live and finished streams of a job or a service.

- It sticks to the bottom while new lines come in.
- Scrolling up pauses that, and a button jumps back to the latest line.
- Filters over its lines go in a `FilterBar` above it, with a `QueryBuilder` for rules.
- `bare` leaves out its card edge and header. A log on its own then runs flush to the view, in a `bare` `Frame`.
