## FilterBar

`FilterBar` is one row that wraps.

1. `search` comes first and grows, from 220 to 420px.
2. Segmented `Tabs`, `Select`s and `Switch`es follow at their width.
3. `end` sits at the right of the first line, however the filters wrap.
   It holds the number of results as a `FilterCount`, or a view switch.

Filters that don't fit wrap onto the next line from the left edge of the bar, under the search field.

`FilterCount` is the bare number in tabular digits.
An icon before it, such as `list` for issues, says what is counted when the list doesn't make it plain.
Screen readers hear its `unit` and the number, such as "Issues: 40".
Never write the count as a sentence, such as "40 issues", in the bar.

The name of a filter is inside the control, as its value or its `aria-label`.
It is never a caption above it.
On a phone the filters scroll sideways under the search field.

## QueryBuilder

`QueryBuilder` filters over the fields of a list, inside the `FilterBar`.

- Each rule is a 30px chip, such as "State is Open" or "Estimate > 3", that opens a popover.
- "Filter" adds a rule.
- The button with the sliders opens the builder, which groups rules with `AND` or `OR`.
- Both buttons come before the chips, so they never wrap onto a line of their own.
- Fields are typed by a schema, `QueryField`.
- `toWhere` and `fromWhere` convert to and from the `where` of the store.
- On a phone it is one sheet of grouped rows.
