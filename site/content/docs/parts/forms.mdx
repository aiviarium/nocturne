## InlineField

`InlineField` is text edited where it is shown.

| Variant | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `title` | A title, 20/28. `multiline` wraps it |
| `text` | A property value, 28px |
| `mono` | An ID or a path |

At rest it reads as text.
On hover it has a `bg-3` fill, and on focus it shows its edge and the focus ring.
Enter commits, Escape reverts, and leaving the field commits.
Do not use a raw `<input>` styled flat.

## Form fields

`TextField`, `TextArea`, `Select`, `Switch` and `SchemaForm` are 30px fields, with the label above in 12px `text-2`.

- A `Switch` applies at once.
- A `Checkbox` is part of a form, with a mixed state for select-all.
- A `RadioGroup` picks one of a few visible options.
- A `Select` is for long or changing sets, such as hosts or scopes. It is a button with `aria-haspopup` and a menu of radio items.

## NumberField

`NumberField` is 30px like the other fields.
`variant="inline"` is 28px, for a `PropertyList` or a table cell.

- It takes sums: `12*4`, `100-15%`, `+5` from the value, and `1.5h` with `units`.
- The arrow keys step. Shift steps by ten, and Alt by a tenth.
- Drag the label, or the field at rest with a mouse, to scrub.
- A click places the caret, and Tab selects the value.
- `steppers` sit stacked at the end on a desktop, and as − and + around the value on a phone.
- The unit is `unit`, after the number, never in the label.
- Input it cannot read keeps the old value and says why.

The arithmetic is `evaluateExpression` in `src/lib/expression.ts`, a parser with no `eval`.

## Slider

`Slider` and `RangeSlider` set a bounded value roughly.

- A 28px row, a 4px track and a 14px thumb, with the value at the right of the label.
- `ticks` and `marks` name the steps.
- `field` adds a field for the exact number.
- On a phone the hit area is 44px and the thumb 22px.

They are not for exact numbers over a large range.

## PropertyList

`PropertyList` shows label and value pairs.

- The label column is 120px, or `labelWidth="auto"`, in 12px `text-3`.
- Values are `text-1`.
- Rows are 28px, so an `InlineField` or a `Select` keeps the row height.
- An empty value is an em dash, never "None".

## SettingRow

`SettingRow` is one setting of a settings page.
Its title, description and key are at the left, and its control is at the right.

- Settings pages have no save button. Each change applies at once, and typed text saves when the field loses focus.
- `modified` draws an accent edge and a "Modified" chip. With `onReset`, the chip resets the value to the one the scope inherits.
- `inherited` says where a value comes from when the scope shown does not set it, such as "From Server".
- `locked` marks a value no scope can change.
- The control is a `Switch`, `Select`, `NumberField` or `TextField` without a label. `control` can be a function that gets the ids of the title and the description, for `aria-labelledby`.
- Put the rows of one plugin or area in a `Card` with `padding="none"`.
