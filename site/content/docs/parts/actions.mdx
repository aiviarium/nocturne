A view's actions are at the right of its header, with the icon actions first and the primary action last.
Actions on one row sit in the row, and rare actions go in a menu.
The shell binds the window's keys, so a view's actions don't bind them again, see [Keyboard](../shell/keyboard.mdx).

## Button

Use a `Button` for an action with words, such as a dialog's submit or the start of an empty state.

| Size | Height | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `md` | `--control-md` | Forms, panels, dialogs |
| `sm` | `--control-sm` | Dense rows, list rows, table bars, toolbars |
| `lg` | `--control-lg` | Empty states |

Every size has `--radius-sm`.

- `primary` is for the one action that completes a flow, such as the last button of a dialog or a form, a sign-in or the answer on a row. It is ink, filled with the text colour, never the accent. Use one for each region.
- `accent` starts AI work.
- `danger` destroys. It has no red edge.
- The starting action of an empty state is a secondary `Button`.
- Labels say what happens.

A ghost `Button` set among text, such as a picker in a table cell above plain values, is `flush`.
Its padding hangs outside its box and it takes the type around it, so its label starts where the text above and below it does.
The roles on <DemoLink to="settings/members">Members</DemoLink> are flush pickers under the owner's plain role.

An action that adds an item is never a `Button`.
It is a ghost plus `IconButton` among the view header's actions: `icon: "plus"` with `primary` left out.
On a phone it is among the trailing actions of the navigation bar.

## IconButton

Use an `IconButton` for an action whose icon says enough, in a header, a row or a toolbar.

- It is `--control-md` square with a 16px icon. `size="sm"` is `--control-sm` with a 13px icon.
- `label` is required. It is the accessible name and the tooltip, so every icon-only control has words.
- It keeps a faint `hover` fill so its hit area shows. A pressed toggle takes the neutral `active` fill, never the accent.
- Never use a bare `<button>` with an icon.

## Menus

Use a `DropdownMenu` for actions or choices behind a button, such as a view's overflow.
Use `RowMenu` for a row's menu, and `ContextMenu` for the right click of any other element.

- `DropdownMenu` takes a `trigger` and `items`, a `MenuEntry[]` of items, separators, labels and choices. It hangs from its trigger and flips when there is no room.
- `Menu` is that menu anchored to an element or a point.
- `RowMenu` and `ContextMenu` open on a right click, a long press, the Menu key or Shift and F10. On a phone they open as a sheet.
- Arrow Up and Down move with wrapping, and Home, End and typing a letter move too. Escape closes the menu and returns focus.
- A checked choice shows a check in the text colour, never the accent.
- Rare actions go behind the overflow button, not in a row of their own.
- A button keeps its current or pressed fill while its menu is open.
- A view header action with `menu` opens that menu from its button, see [View header](../shell/view-header.mdx#actions).

## Tooltip

A tooltip shows the label of a control or explains a mark, such as an `IconButton`'s label with its shortcut.
`IconButton` draws its own.
Use `Tooltip` for any other element that needs one.
Tooltips are dark in both themes, and phones don't show them.

## Kbd

`Kbd` is one key cap, and `KbdGroup` a few in a row, such as ⌘ and K.
They're mono and never wrap.
Use them to show a shortcut in a list of shortcuts.
Menus and tooltips draw their own.

The demo's <DemoLink to="settings/shortcuts">Keyboard shortcuts</DemoLink> use them.

## AccountMenu

`AccountMenu` is the signed-in person's menu, at the foot of the sidebar.
`Shell` draws it from its `account` prop.

- Its trigger is a row with the avatar and the name. On the sidebar's icon rail it is the avatar alone, and `trigger="avatar"` gives the same anywhere else.
- At the top of the menu is the signed-in person, with `detail` and `badge` beside the name.
- The app's `items` follow, then "Appearance…" with `onAppearance`, which opens the [appearance settings](../shell/appearance.mdx).
- With `theme`, the menu has one theme row, where System, Light and Dark are three icons.
- With `onSignOut`, the last item signs out.

A choice with `variant="icons"` is that same row for another setting: its label at the start and its options as icons at the end.
