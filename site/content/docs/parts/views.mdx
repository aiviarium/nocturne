## The frame of a view

A view is `ViewLayout` with a `ShellViewHeader` inside the [app shell](../shell/index.mdx).
The header is 44px, like every header in the window.
Its title or trail is at the drill size, 12px/500, the size of the title bar's path, so nothing changes size as the person drills.
The view area is raised, square, and runs to the window's bottom edge.

Header actions come in one order:

1. icon actions
2. pin
3. overflow
4. the primary action

The header's last crumb, or its title, names the view's tab through `setTitle` on the app's `ViewChrome`.
A view switch or search goes in the header's middle, not in a toolbar under it.

<DoDont do="design/compact-header" dont="design/inflated-header" />

At a place the header shows the place's name.
At a record it shows the trail, `Issues / WEB-142 ⌄`, with the record crumb as a switcher and the stepper at the right.
There is no back chevron.
[View header and trail](../shell/view-header.mdx) has the rules.

### Width

Every view in an app uses the same width, so moving between them doesn't move the content.
The main column fills its width, and its content starts at its left edge.
Lists, tables, logs, forms and overviews all follow this rule.
A form lays out its fields in columns inside its sections instead of narrowing the view.

`reading` is only for long prose, such as a document's body or an article.
It centres the column at a reading width.
Don't use it for forms or settings: one centred page among full-width pages reads as a different app.

## Card

A `Card` is a box of content.
It has 12px padding (`--nc-pad`), radius 8, a `line-soft` edge and a `bg-1` fill.
A `nested` card is `bg-2`.

- A card is as tall as its content.
- `fill` makes it use a box that a grid gives it, and the body takes the height. A `RowList` scrolls inside it and an `EmptyState` centres in it. A card with one `Stat` keeps its label and figure at the top. The figure is 32px in a tall box, and its chart takes the rest.
- `title`, `count` and `action` put a `SectionHeading` inside.
- `padding="none"` is for media or tables that reach the edges.
- `href` or `onClick` make the whole card one link.
- A card inside a card, or a list in a card, does not add a second padding.

## Panel

A `Panel` is a tool region with a header.
The header is 44px, with the title, a `state` and `actions`.
The body has 12px padding.

- `footer` is only for totals or pagination.
- `fill` works as for `Card`.
- `flush` is for a table or a terminal.

## Section heading

`SectionHeading` is the one treatment for a section inside a view or card.
It is 13px semibold `text-1`, in a 24px row.

- `count` is a total in `text-3`.
- One `action` goes at the right. It is a ghost `Button size="sm"`, an `IconButton size="sm"`, a link or a `State`.
- It is never 16, 20 or 26px.
- Group labels in a sidebar or menu are the nav label, 11px in `text-3`. They are not section headings.

## Layout parts

- `AppShell` lays out the window, with the title bar, the sidebar, the view area and its docked panels, and the panel rail. `Shell` fills it from a `createShell` registry. See [The app shell](../shell/index.mdx).
- `Frame` has a `TabStrip` header.
- `Split` and `SplitGroup` have dividers you can drag. A divider is a focusable separator. The arrow keys, Home and End move it. A double click resets it.
- `Dock` tiles frames, each with a `TabStrip`: columns side by side, frames stacked in a column. It is a view's content, never a part of the shell, and the shell's panels have no tiling. Dragging a tab onto another frame's strip moves it there. Dropping it on a side of a frame splits that frame. The layout is data. `dockLayout`, `dockAdd`, `dockSplit`, `dockRemove` and `dockSync` change it, and the app keeps it.
- `Dialog` is centred, bare, near the top, or a bottom sheet.
- `LinkProvider` sends `NavItem` and `Button` links through your router.
