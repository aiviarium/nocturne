---
title: Overview
description: The part to use for each thing a view shows.
---

Build every view from these parts.
Views then look and behave alike, however many teams or modules write them.
The window around them is the [app shell](../shell/index.mdx).

Do not restyle a part for one view.
If a part lacks something, add a variant to it.

Sizes are the tokens in `src/styles/tokens.css`: `--control-*`, `--row-*`, `--header`, `--space-*` and `--radius-*`.
On a phone each part takes the phone scale by itself, see [Phones](../phones.mdx).
