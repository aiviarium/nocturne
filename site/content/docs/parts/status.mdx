## State

`State` is a dot and a word, everywhere.

- The word has a colour only for warn and error.
- Only `live` and `ai` glow.
- A row has one, in the `status` of a `ListRow`.
- Show it only when it is out of the ordinary. No "Live", "Idle" or "Ready" on every row.

## Badge, LabelChip and Count

- A `Badge` is for a kind, such as "Draft" or "Admin", or a category. It never shows a state that a `State` shows.
- A label on a card, row or table, such as "Bug", is a toned `Badge`. It is more compact than a chip and fits the record.
  Give it a status tone only when the label means one, such as `danger` for a bug. Other labels stay `neutral`.
- A `LabelChip` is a tag that stands alone, such as a project or area in a picker, in one of the label colours.
- `Count` is an attention count, drawn in the accent. Totals are plain numbers in `text-3`.

One list never has two treatments for the same fact.

## MetaLine

`MetaLine` is one line of facts about an item, in 12px `text-3`, joined by " · ".
Empty parts are dropped.
It is cut at the end, with the full line as its title.
It has no state, and nothing that is always true, such as "Created today".

## EmptyState

`EmptyState` is `md` for a whole view or main column, and `sm` in a card, panel or section.
Only a whole view, or a list the person filtered, gets one.
An empty part of a view is not drawn.

## Truncate

`Truncate` is one line, or `lines`, with an ellipsis and the full text as its title.
Every part on these pages that cuts text already does this, such as row titles, meta lines and crumbs.

## Other display parts

- `Avatar` is initials in a circle for people, a rounded square for agents, and the sparkle mark for the assistant.
- `Callout` is an inline message in a neutral grey box. The hue is in its icon. It has one or two sentences: what happened and what to do next.
- `Toast` shows short notices in a live region at the bottom right. Danger toasts are announced assertively, and hover pauses the timer.
- `Skeleton` holds the place of content while it loads.
