These parts mark facts about an item.
Indicators mark the unusual.
A state shows while something is wrong or running, and a count while it is above zero.
Each fact appears once on a screen.
In the shell these rules apply to the sidebar, the title bar's status items and every panel too.

## State and StatusDot

Use `State` for the state of an item: a dot and a word, everywhere.
`StatusDot` is the dot alone, for a place where the word is already beside it, such as a list of runs.

- The word has a colour only for warn and error.
- Only `live` and `ai` glow.
- A row has one, in the `status` of a `ListRow`.
- Show it only when it is out of the ordinary. No "Live", "Idle" or "Ready" on every row.

## Badge, LabelChip and Count

- A `Badge` is for a kind, such as "Draft" or "Admin", or a category. It never shows a state that a `State` shows.
- A label on a card, row or table, such as "Bug", is a toned `Badge`. It is more compact than a chip and fits the record.
  Give it a status tone only when the label means one, such as `danger` for a bug. Other labels stay `neutral`.
- A `LabelChip` is a tag outside a record, such as a project or area in a picker, in one of the label colours.
- `Count` is an attention count on a neutral fill, never the accent. In the sidebar, counts and totals are plain numbers in `text-3`.

One list never has two treatments for the same fact.

## IdBadge and CodeBadge

`IdBadge` is an identifier such as `WEB-142`, always mono.
`CodeBadge` is an error code from a call, such as `409`, which leads a callout or a cell when the call was refused.

A failing delivery of <DemoLink to="settings/webhooks/deploys-slack">a webhook</DemoLink> shows its code.

## MetaLine

`MetaLine` is one line of facts about an item, in 12px `text-3`, joined by " · ".
Empty parts are dropped.
It is cut at the end, with the full line as its title.
It has no state, and nothing that is always true, such as "Created today".
Its parts are values, such as `main`, `Mira Okonkwo` or `3h`. A phrase such as "updated 3h ago" is not a value.
Use it in a row or a card. Under a title, or at the top of a page or panel, it would be a subtitle.
A record's facts there are a `PropertyList`.

## EmptyState

`EmptyState` is `md` for a whole view or main column, and `sm` in a card, panel or section.
Only a whole view, or a list the person filtered, gets one.
An empty part of a view isn't drawn.
It has a short title, the action that starts it, and one short line at most.

## Callout

`Callout` is an inline message in a neutral grey box, with its hue in the icon only.
It has one or two sentences, which say what happened and what to do next.
Use it for a problem with what the view shows, such as a failing webhook, not for help text.

## Skeleton

`Skeleton` holds the place of content while it loads.
It fades in after 400ms, so a load that finishes sooner shows nothing in between.
`immediate` shows it at once, where the wait is known to be long.
Show it only while there is nothing to show, never on a refresh of data already on screen.

## Avatar and Mark

- `Avatar` is initials in a circle for people, a rounded square for agents, and the sparkle mark for the assistant.
- `AvatarStack` overlaps a few avatars, such as the voters of an idea.
- `Mark` is the mark of a place, such as a workspace, a space, a project or the product. It is a rounded square with the first letter, an icon or a logo. Use `sm` (16px) in the path, switchers and crumbs, `md` (20px), the default, in rows and cards, and `lg` (32px) on a page. `solid` fills it, for the product's own mark.

These keep their hues, because the colour tells people and places apart.
