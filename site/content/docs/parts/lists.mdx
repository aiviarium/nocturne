Use `RowList` with `ListRow` for a list of things.
A table of columns is a `DataTable`, see [Records](./records.mdx#datatable).

## Sizes

| Setting | Row height |
| --- | --- |
| `density="compact"`, the default | 30px |
| `density="comfortable"` | 36px |
| `lines={2}` or `lines={3}` | Rows with more text |
| On a phone | 44px for one line, 58px for two |

A row of two or three lines has its title at 13/20 and its secondary text at 12/16, with 6px vertical padding.
The time ends the first line, and the state ends the second.

## The parts of a row

In order:

1. `icon` (16px) or `leading` (an `Avatar size="sm"`), in a fixed 20px box
2. `title`
3. `secondary`
4. `status`, one `State`
5. `meta`, a time or a count in `text-3`
6. `actions` or `persistentActions`

`actions` are `IconButton size="sm"`.
They show on hover, focus and selection.
On a phone or a touch screen they take no width, and are a left swipe and a long press menu away.
`persistentActions` are always shown, such as answer buttons.

`leadingActions` and `trailingActions` are swipe actions.
`menu` is the context menu.

## Columns line up

The rows of a `RowList` share one grid, each row a subgrid.
Icons, titles, states, times and actions line up down the list.

The title keeps the width of the longest title, up to 40% of the row, before the trailing columns take theirs.
The secondary text gives way first.

## Row states

- `selected` is the inset fill, `bg-4`, 6px from the edge of the list.
- `unread` bolds the title and puts a dot at the leading edge of the row, inside the fill. In a list that tracks it, pass `unread={false}` on the read rows, so the column of the dot stays and nothing moves.
- `muted` dims the row.
- `movable={false}` pins a row in a list with `onReorder`.

No row has a grip or a delete button.
You drag the row itself, and Delete is in its menu and on the Delete key.

## Links and long lists

With `href` or `onClick` the whole row is one link or button.

`RowList items={…}` renders only the visible rows past 150 rows.
Give it a height.
Its rows then take the widest trailing cell seen so far for each column.

`divided` adds hairlines between rows of several lines.

## Variants

`density` and `variant` belong to the list.

| Variant | Where |
| --- | --- |
| `inset` | In a card, panel or inspector at any depth. Titles sit on the text edge of the container, and the fill is 6px from its edge |
| `inline` | In a property value or a form. 28px rows on the edge of the content |
| `boxed` | A group of its own |

## Keys and gestures

The arrow keys, Home and End move between rows.
The list is one tab stop.

With `items` and `getKey` a list can do more.

- `onReorder(from, to)` moves rows. Drag the row itself past 4px, or on touch move it after the long press that opened its menu. Alt and an arrow key move it too. A line shows where it drops, and the list scrolls at its edges. The indices are those of `items`, so this works on long lists.
- `selectable` lets the person select rows. A click opens, Cmd or Ctrl and a click toggles, and Shift and a click selects a range. Shift and an arrow key extend it. Space, Cmd or Ctrl and A, and Escape work as usual. A bar at the foot shows only while something is selected, with `bulkActions`.
- `onRemove` removes rows, by the Delete key, Delete in the menu, or a swipe. Return an undo for a toast.
- `onAdd` adds an add row.
- `editing` is the iOS edit mode: a selection circle, then the content.
- `rowMenu` is the context menu. It opens with a right click, the Menu key, Shift and F10, or a long press. The default items are Open, the row's actions, Rename and Delete.

On a row of a selection of several, the menu acts on the selection, with the bar's actions and "Delete 3".
Any other row is selected alone first.

A row's `onRename` edits its title in place, by F2 or a double click.
A row's `children` nest under it as a tree.
