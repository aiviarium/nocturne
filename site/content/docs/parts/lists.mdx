Use `RowList` with `ListRow` for a list of things with one title each, such as issues, messages or files.
Use a `DataTable` when the person compares columns, see [Records](./records.mdx#datatable).

In the shell a list fills the view, under the view header.
Selecting a row shows it in the Details panel and leaves the trail as it is.
Opening a row goes to the record, and the header shows its trail and the stepper over this list, in its order and filter.

## Sizes

| Setting | Row height |
| --- | --- |
| One line, the only desktop row height | 30px |
| `lines={2}` or `lines={3}` | Rows with more text |
| On a phone | 44px for one line, 58px for two |

A row of two or three lines has its title at 13/20 and its secondary text at 12/16, with 6px vertical padding.
The time ends the first line, and the state ends the second.

## The parts of a row

In order:

1. `icon` (16px) or `leading` (an `Avatar size="sm"`), in a fixed 20px box. A row of a record draws the record's `EntryGlyph`, see below
2. `prefix`, short facts before the title on its line, such as an issue's key and its state icon
3. `title`
4. `secondary`
5. `status`, one `State`
6. `meta`, a time or a count in `text-3`
7. `actions` or `persistentActions`

The prefix is quiet text at the label size, with tabular figures, so keys of one length line up from row to row.
It stays on the title's line, and the two shrink together.
A state glyph in it is an `Icon` with a `tone`: `success`, `warning`, `danger`, `info`, or `quiet` for `text-3`.

`actions` are `IconButton size="sm"`.
They show on hover, focus and selection.
On a phone or a touch screen they take no width, and are a left swipe and a long press menu away.
`persistentActions` are always shown, such as answer buttons.

A record looks the same in its view's list as in the sidebar.
Build its `ShellRecord` in one function, give it to the place's `records`, and draw the row's `leading` as `<EntryGlyph {...record} />`.
The row then has the same identicon or avatar, the same badge and the same hover card as the entry.
Leave out `status` on the glyph when the row writes the state out at its end.
A click on a glyph with a hover card still opens the row.

`leadingActions` and `trailingActions` are swipe actions.
`menu` is the context menu.

## Columns line up

The rows of a `RowList` share one grid, each row a subgrid.
Icons, titles, states, times and actions line up down the list.

The title keeps the width of the longest title, up to 40% of the row, before the trailing columns take theirs.
The secondary text gives way first.

## Row states

- `selected` is the faint inset fill, `active`, 6px from the edge of the list, with a step up in text colour to `text-1`. The size and weight stay, and there is no edge bar.
- Hover only lightens the quiet text and the icons, with no fill.
- `unread` bolds the title and puts a dot in `text-2` at the leading edge of the row, inside the fill. The dot is neutral, never the accent. In a list that tracks it, pass `unread={false}` on the read rows, so the column of the dot stays and nothing moves.
- `muted` dims the row.
- `movable={false}` pins a row in a list with `onReorder`.

No row has a grip or a delete button.
You drag the row itself, and Delete is in its menu and on the Delete key.

## RowSection

`RowSection` groups rows under a heading inside a `RowList`, such as issues by state.
With `items`, give the list `group`, the group of each item in group order, and `section` for each group's heading.
The rows stay the list's own, so selection, the row menu, removal and the keys work across the groups.
A grouped list isn't virtualised.
The heading shows the group's name at the body size, with its count, an optional icon or `leading` glyph, and buttons that show on hover and focus.
Its chevron folds the group to its heading, and `expanded` or `defaultExpanded` set whether the rows show.
The rows keep the list's shared columns and its keyboard model, so they line up across groups, and the arrow keys move from one group to the next.
Group heads have no fill, and groups are set apart by space.

## Links and long lists

With `href` or `onClick` the whole row is one link or button.

`RowList items={…}` renders only the visible rows past 150 rows.
Give it a height.
Its rows then take the widest trailing cell seen so far for each column.

`divided` adds hairlines between rows of several lines.

## Variants

`variant` belongs to the list.

| Variant | Where |
| --- | --- |
| `inset` | In a card or panel at any depth, such as the Details panel. Titles sit on the text edge of the container, and the fill is 6px from its edge |
| `inline` | In a property value or a form. 28px rows on the edge of the content |
| `boxed` | A group of its own |

## Keys and gestures

The arrow keys, Home and End move between rows.
The list is one tab stop.

With `items` and `getKey` a list can do more.

Rows are kept by `getKey`. An item replaced with the same key keeps its row, which shows the new item. A new key adds a row.

- `onReorder(from, to)` moves rows. Drag the row itself past 4px, or on touch move it after the long press that opened its menu. Alt and an arrow key move it too. A line shows where it drops, and the list scrolls at its edges. The indices are those of `items`, so this works on long lists.
- `selectable` lets the person select rows. A click opens, Cmd or Ctrl and a click toggles, and Shift and a click selects a range. Shift and an arrow key extend it. Space, Cmd or Ctrl and A, and Escape work as usual. A bar at the foot shows only while something is selected, with `bulkActions`.
- `onRemove` removes rows, by the Delete key, Delete in the menu, or a swipe. Return an undo for a toast.
- `onAdd` adds an add row.
- `editing` is the iOS edit mode: a selection circle, then the content.
- `rowMenu` is the context menu. It opens with a right click, the Menu key, Shift and F10, or a long press. The default items are Open, the row's actions, Rename and Delete.

On a row of a selection of several, the menu acts on the selection, with the bar's actions and "Delete 3".
Any other row is selected alone first.

A row's `onRename` edits its title in place, by F2 or a double click.
A row's `children` nest under it as a tree.

## List

`List` is a long list of one-line rows of one fixed height, rendered only where visible.
Use it for a run log or a picker with thousands of entries.
It is a listbox, where the arrow keys, Home, End, Page Up and Page Down move, and Enter picks.
`multiselectable` with `isSelected` lets more than one row be selected.
Use a `RowList` when rows have a title, facts and actions.
