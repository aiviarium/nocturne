A figure reports a number once, such as the issues closed in a cycle or the deploys this week.
It goes in a card on an overview or a dashboard, not in a view header or a row.
A value that changes while the person watches is a widget, see [Widgets](./widgets.mdx).

## Stat and StatCard

A figure is a `Stat` in a `Card`, or a `StatCard`.

- The value is 24px with tabular digits, or 32px when it fills a tall box.
- The unit is 12px `text-3`, and the label 12px `text-2`, with a signal at the right of the label.
- In a box it fills, the label and figure stay at the top. A chart (`WidgetSparkline`) or a meter takes the rest of the height. There is never a gap above the figure. This holds for a `fill` card with one `Stat` and for `StatCard fill`.

Cards in a row share edges and baselines.

## Delta

`Delta` is a change since last time: an arrow and tabular numbers.
Up is good and down is bad by default, in the success and danger colours.
Set `sentiment` when lower is better, such as for open issues or an error rate.
Put it in the `signal` of a `Stat`, at the right of the label.
The open issues on the <DemoLink to="dashboard">Dashboard</DemoLink> go down, which is good.
