## Stat and StatCard

A figure is a `Stat` in a `Card`, or a `StatCard`.

- The value is 24px with tabular digits, or 32px when it fills a tall box.
- The unit is 12px `text-3`, and the label 12px `text-2`, with a signal at the right of the label.
- In a box it fills, the label and figure stay at the top. A chart (`WidgetSparkline`) or a meter takes the rest of the height. There is never a gap above the figure. This holds for a `fill` card with one `Stat` and for `StatCard fill`.

Cards in a row share edges and baselines.

## Widget

A `Widget` shows a live value.

- Radius 20.
- One hue, the accent or a status colour. Only the moving part has the hue and its glow.
- With `fill` in a grid cell, the title is at the top and a sparkline or list grows to fill the cell. A bar, strip or gauge goes at the bottom with the caption.

## WidgetGrid

`WidgetGrid` is a page of widgets: 12 columns of 80px rows.
While `editing`, items drag and resize.

Outside editing it draws the stored layout compacted.

- Items in `empty`, with nothing to show, take no space.
- Each item is as tall as its content, up to its size. This is `fit`, on by default.
- Items of one height on one row end on one line.
- Everything moves up.

Cards on a `WidgetGrid` take the widget frame, with 18 or 20px padding and radius 20.
