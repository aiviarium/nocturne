## Where they mount

Popovers, menus, dialogs, tooltips and toasts mount inside the nearest element with a `data-theme`.
They inherit its theme and `--accent`.
With no such element they fall back to `document.body`, in a `div.nc` that copies the theme of the opener and keeps it in step.

## The order

Overlays stack in a fixed order, lowest first:

1. the phone drawer
2. the scrim of a dialog
3. popovers
4. tooltips
5. toasts

A `Select`, menu or tooltip opened in a `Dialog` shows above it.
Escape closes only the topmost open layer.

## Popover

A `Popover` is a box that floats next to an anchor element.
It flips to the other side when there is no room and stays inside the viewport.
Escape or a press outside it closes it.

## Dialog

A `Dialog` is centred, bare, near the top, or a bottom sheet.
Focus moves in when it opens, Tab cycles inside, and Escape closes it.
Focus then returns to the button that opened it.

## Tooltips and toasts

A tooltip shows the label of an `IconButton` with its shortcut.

Toasts are short notices in a live region at the bottom right.
Danger toasts are announced assertively, and hover pauses the timer.
Put a `ToastRegion` next to the root of the app.
