## Built on ui-sdk

Popovers, menus, selects, dialogs, sheets, tooltips and toasts are built on [ui-sdk](https://ui-sdk.dev).
ui-sdk does the keyboard, focus, dismissal, placement and ARIA, and Nocturne draws them with its `nc-*` classes.
The caller keeps `open` or `value` and changes it in `onClose` or `onChange`.

## Where they mount

An overlay renders in the top layer of the browser, at the place in the page where you put it.
It inherits the theme and `--accent` of that place, so a menu opened in a light panel is light.
The accent of a lens is set on `<html>`, so overlays take it wherever they mount.
The menu of a table row is the one exception: it renders next to the table, in a `div.nc` that copies the table's theme.

## The order

The top layer stacks overlays in the order they open, so a `Select`, menu or tooltip opened in a `Dialog` shows above it.
Escape closes only the topmost open overlay.
A modal dialog or sheet makes the page behind it inert until it closes.

## Motion

Menus, popovers, the palette, dialogs and toasts fade in over `--dur-fade`, 90ms.
They are the only parts that fade.
A view, a panel or a list that swaps its content does it in the same frame.
Under reduced motion nothing fades.

## Popover

A `Popover` is a box that floats next to an anchor element.
It flips to the other side when there is no room and stays inside the viewport.
Escape or a press outside it closes it.
For a button that opens a popover of its own, use `PopoverButton`.

## Dialog

Use a `Dialog` for what a new item needs, or to confirm a step that can't be undone.
It takes `open`, `onClose`, `title`, `description`, `footer` and `size`, with the primary button last in the footer.
A form with unsaved input passes `closeOnBackdrop={false}`.
A dialog is centred, bare, near the top, or a bottom sheet.
Focus moves in when it opens, Tab cycles inside, and Escape closes it.
Focus then returns to the button that opened it.
On a phone a dialog with a title is a `Sheet`, which rests at the height of its content and is dragged down to close.
A short choice or a destructive confirmation on a phone is an `ActionSheet`, see [Phones](./phones.mdx#sheets-and-lists).

## Tooltips and toasts

A tooltip shows the label of an `IconButton` with its shortcut.
Tooltips are dark in both themes: `bg-3` with `text-1` in dark, and `text-1` with `bg-0` text in light.

Toasts are short notices in a live region at the bottom right.
Danger toasts are announced assertively, and hover or focus pauses the timer.
F6 moves focus into the region and back.
Put one `ToastRegion` next to the root of the app, and call `toast({ title, description, tone, action })`.
